# App Name: Candidates (Q21)

> **Decided 2026-10-23: JaxSide** ("The JaxSide app", D118). The rest of this file is the record of what was considered.

**Still for the owner to do:** register jaxside.com and jaxside.app; search the USPTO trademark database (tmsearch.uspto.gov) for `jaxside` and `jax side` (the site blocks automated searches); claim @jaxside on Instagram, TikTok, X and Facebook (those sites can't be checked from here). Checked already: no app named JaxSide on the App Store or Google Play (2026-10-23).

The app's name was open; "Jax" was a placeholder. The icon is chosen: the art deco emblem of the Modis
Building and the Main Street Bridge (`apps/mobile/assets/brand/icon-emblem.svg`). Everything that depends on
the name (domain, email sender, app store ID, listings, legal pages) waits for the decision.

Domain status: checked against the official .com (Verisign) and .app (Google Registry) records. "Free" means
nobody had registered it on that date, not that it's reserved. Register the final name's domains promptly.

## Avoid

- "First Coast …": First Coast News is a local TV station.
- "DUUUVAL": associated with the Jaguars' game-day chant.
- Team names, logos and marks (Jaguars, NFL).

## Before deciding (for the final one or two)

1. U.S. trademark search (USPTO) for the name and close variants.
2. App Store and Google Play: no app with the same or a confusingly similar name.
3. Social handles: Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook.
4. Register the .com (and .app) the same day.
5. Then: app display name and store ID (`app.json`), email sender and SES domain, legal pages, store listings
   (`docs/STORE_PREP.md`), domain in `docs/LAUNCH_SERVICES.md`.

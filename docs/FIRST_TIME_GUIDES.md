# First-time guides (draft)

User idea, 2026-10-19. **Not built yet**: tracked as Phase 11 in `PROJECT_PLAN.md`, with open
questions in Q18. This file holds the drafted wording so it can be reviewed before anything is
built.

## How it works

The first time someone does one of the actions below, a short guide pops up before they can
continue:

- **Pages:** swipe or tap **Next**. Dots at the bottom show how many pages there are and which one
  you're on.
  1. **Do's:** green check marks.
  2. **Don'ts:** red X marks.
  3. **Good to know:** privacy, what happens if rules are broken, where to read more.
- **The last page** has one button, "Got it" (or "Got it, let's post"). Closing the guide early
  cancels the action. Nothing is lost: the guide shows again next time until it's finished.
- **Once per account, not per phone.** After "Got it" that guide never appears again, even on a
  new phone or after reinstalling. If a guide's rules change in an important way, it can be shown
  once more (each guide has a version number).
- **Always re-readable:** Account → **Community rules** lists every guide, and each composer has
  a small "Rules" link.
- **Short:** at most 3–4 pages and about 5 bullets per page. Pages scroll at large text sizes.
- **Not the rulebook:** the guides summarize. The binding text stays in the Terms of Service and
  Community Guidelines, and every rule a guide mentions must also be in those documents. Ideally
  the wording comes from one shared file, so the guides and the policies can't drift apart.
- **Accessible:** screen readers announce "Page 1 of 3", the check and X icons have spoken
  labels ("Do", "Don't"), and animations respect the phone's reduce-motion setting. The planned
  mascot (Future ideas) could later host these guides.

### Shared facts used below

These are true today; check them again when building.

- **What others see:** your username, profile photo, account color, medals, bio, and roughly how
  far away a post was made ("120 yd").
- **What nobody sees:** your email, birth year or exact location. Photos and voice clips are
  cleaned of location and device details before anyone sees them.
- **Moderators:** they can see account details when they review a report.
- **Automatic checks:** the app checks text for language that isn't allowed and may warn you or
  block the post.
- **Penalties:** warning → 1-day suspension → 7-day suspension → permanent ban. Each step stops
  counting after 6 months, serious cases can skip steps, and a person reviews every step.
  Warnings and suspensions can be appealed.

## 1. First area post (general feed)

**Do**

- ✅ Share what's happening around here: events, news, things you noticed nearby.
- ✅ Ask your neighbors: recommendations, lost and found, "does anyone know…".
- ✅ Give a heads-up: road closures, power outages, weather, safety notices.
- ✅ Keep it local: it should matter to people within a few blocks.
- ✅ Be kind and assume good intent.

**Don't**

- ❌ Post mostly about one specific business. Reviews and detailed talk about a place go in that
  place's discussion.
- ❌ Bully, harass, threaten, or use racist, hateful or demeaning language.
- ❌ Share anyone's personal information: names with addresses, phone numbers, license plates,
  photos of their home. This is called "doxxing".
- ❌ Post sexual, explicit or graphic violent content.
- ❌ Spam or advertise: repeat posts, sales pitches, links to make money.
- ❌ Post something dangerous or illegal. If someone is in danger, call 911 first.

**Good to know**

- You're **pseudonymous**. People see your username and photo, never your email or exact
  location. It's your choice whether to tell anyone who you really are.
- Only people within your radius see area posts.
- Breaking the rules can lead to a warning, then suspensions, then a ban.
- Full rules: Account → Community rules.

## 2. First place post (business discussion)

**Do**

- ✅ Talk about this place: your experience, tips, what's new, the best thing to order.
- ✅ Be honest and fair, good or bad.
- ✅ Share useful details: hours you noticed, parking, wait times, accessibility.
- ✅ Post while you're actually near it. That's what makes these discussions trustworthy.

**Don't**

- ❌ Post fake reviews, or review a place you haven't been to.
- ❌ Name, describe or post photos of staff or other customers in a way that singles them out.
- ❌ Post about unrelated topics. Those belong in the area feed.
- ❌ Advertise for a competitor, or post the same promotion over and over.
- ❌ Break any of the area post rules: no harassment, hate, explicit content or personal info.

**Good to know**

- Place discussions open only within about 528 yards of the place.
- **Own or work at this place?** Say so in your post. Owners can get a verified **Owner** tag (see Q19). Repeated promotions aren't allowed.

## 3. First comment or reply _(suggested addition: short, one page plus "Good to know")_

**Do**

- ✅ Add to the conversation: answer, agree or add something useful.
- ✅ Disagree with ideas, not people.

**Don't**

- ❌ Insult, pile on, or reply just to provoke.
- ❌ Repeat the same comment across posts.
- ❌ Share anyone's personal information.

**Good to know**

- Commenting and voting need you within about 528 yards of the post.
- If a thread gets heated, you can leave it, or block someone without them being told.

## 4. First photo in a post _(suggested addition)_

**Do**

- ✅ Share photos you took of things around the area.

**Don't**

- ❌ Post close-ups of people who didn't agree to be photographed, especially children.
- ❌ Show license plates, house numbers, or screenshots of private messages.
- ❌ Post explicit, graphic or copyrighted images.

**Good to know**

- Photos are cleaned of location and camera details before anyone sees them.
- Long-pressing a photo lets others report it.

## 5. First voice post _(suggested addition)_

**Do**

- ✅ Talk about what's happening nearby, in your own voice. Up to 60 seconds.

**Don't**

- ❌ Record other people's conversations without them knowing.
- ❌ Play music or other audio you don't own.
- ❌ Say anything that would break the text rules. Voice posts are reviewed when someone reports
  them.

**Good to know**

- Background sounds can give away where you are. Listen back before you post.

## 6. First place suggestion (add a place)

**Do**

- ✅ Suggest real public places: businesses, parks, venues, community spots.
- ✅ Stand at the place when you suggest it. Your current location becomes its spot on the map.
- ✅ Use the place's real name and the closest matching category.
- ✅ Check the "Already listed nearby?" matches first.

**Don't**

- ❌ Suggest private homes or people's addresses.
- ❌ Suggest places that don't exist, joke entries, or duplicates.
- ❌ Put ads or contact details in the name or notes.

**Good to know**

- People nearby vote on it under **Verify changes near you** in Places: 10 "It's here" votes add it
  (yours counts first), 10 "Not here" votes drop it, and it expires after 60 days without a
  result (D94). Added suggestions earn the **Approved Contributor** medal.
- Only the place's location is saved with it, not yours.

## 7. First "closed / doesn't exist" vote (remove a place)

**Do**

- ✅ Vote when a place has closed for good, moved, or was never there.
- ✅ Be at or near the spot so you can see for yourself.

**Don't**

- ❌ Vote to remove a place because you had a bad experience. Post about it instead.
- ❌ Vote against a competitor or a place you dislike.
- ❌ Ask others to mass-vote.

**Good to know**

- You can vote once on each place, and you can't change it.
- 10 "Closed" votes from people nearby take a place off Places; 10 "Still open" votes keep it.
- Every vote is kept with who voted, when and how far away. Admins review unusual voting, can
  restore a place, and misuse counts as breaking the rules.

## 8. First report

**Do**

- ✅ Report posts, comments, photos or profiles that break the Community Guidelines.
- ✅ Pick the closest reason and add details if they help.
- ✅ Block someone if you just don't want to see them. That's not what reports are for.

**Don't**

- ❌ Report just because you disagree with someone.
- ❌ Organize group reports, or report the same thing from several accounts.
- ❌ Use reports to get back at someone. Misusing reports is against the rules.

**Good to know**

- The person isn't told who reported them. A moderator reviews every report, and you can follow
  yours in Account → My reports.
- If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

## 9. First profile change (bio, username, photo) _(one guide for all three)_

**Do**

- ✅ Pick a username and photo you're comfortable with neighbors seeing.
- ✅ Keep your bio friendly. A line about your interests or neighborhood is plenty.

**Don't**

- ❌ Put your phone number, home address, email or social handles in your bio or name. It
  defeats the privacy that keeps you safe here.
- ❌ Pretend to be someone else, a business, the city, or the police.
- ❌ Use offensive, hateful or explicit names, bios or photos.

**Good to know**

- Usernames can be changed once every 30 days, and your old name stays reserved for a while so
  no one can pretend to be you.
- Your bio, photo, username and color are visible to anyone who opens your profile.

## 10. First friend request _(suggested addition, Phase 9 is built)_

**Good to know** (one page)

- Friends see "@you posted near [neighborhood]" in their friends feed, and which places you've
  favorited.
- You can hide your recent posts from friends in Account.
- Only send requests to people you know or have talked with. Repeated unwanted requests count as
  harassment.

## 11. First block _(suggested addition)_

**Good to know** (one page)

- They won't see your profile, and you won't see their posts or comments.
- They aren't told.
- Undo it any time in Account → Blocked users.
- Blocking doesn't report them. If they broke a rule, report them too.

## 12. Guest welcome _(suggested addition: first launch as a guest)_

**Good to know** (one page)

- As a guest you can read posts and comments nearby.
- Posting, commenting, voting, reporting and place discussions need a free account.
- Your guest session ends after a while without use.

## 13. First private message _(with Phase 10)_

**Do**

- ✅ Message people you've met through the app: follow-ups, plans, questions.

**Don't**

- ❌ Send unwanted explicit images or messages, or keep messaging someone who hasn't replied.
- ❌ Scam, sell or ask for money, passwords or personal details.
- ❌ Share screenshots of other people's private messages.

**Good to know**

- You can block or report from any conversation.
- Moderators can read messages that are reported to them.
- Meeting someone in person? Pick a public place and tell a friend.
- (The final wording depends on the Phase 10 design: retention, whether strangers can message
  you, and so on.)

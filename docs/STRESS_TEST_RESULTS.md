## What this means (written 2026-10-26)

- **It holds up.** Two full runs of 1,000 active people (about 156,000 requests each) had
  **no errors**. The busiest hour of that day needs about 6.5 requests a second; this PC
  handled 100–140. A Lightsail 2 GB server is slower (2 shared cores), but even at a fifth of
  this speed it has several times the headroom, and the first months are expected at a few
  hundred people, a fraction of this day.
- **Times under load are mostly waiting in line.** Alone, every read is 14–33 ms and every
  write 40–70 ms; under the compressed day (24 requests at once, a whole day in 20–25 minutes)
  the medians rise to 100–500 ms. Real traffic is far more spread out.
- **Votes were the slowest common write.** Each upvote re-checked all of the author's medals
  (about 8 queries). Now it checks only the medals a vote can change (P15.12): 73 → 58 ms
  alone. The nightly job still checks everything.
- **Half of all requests are the two badge checks** (Messages and the bell, every 30 s while
  the app is open). Combining them into one request would halve that traffic and its
  rate-limit counters. Not done yet (your call).
- **Photos cost the most CPU** (about 300 ms alone to check and re-encode each one). Fine at
  this volume; on a small server many uploads at once queue behind each other.
- **Storage:** about 15 MB of lasting database growth a day (5 GB a year, less after the
  retention jobs) and about 90 MB of photos, voice and video a day (about 2.7 GB a month).
  Rate-limit counters add about 50 MB a day but are deleted each night, so they don't add up.
- **Costs at this volume:** the database fits on the server's included disk; S3 storage
  starts at pennies and reaches about $1 a month after a year; downloads (about 200 GB a
  month) are well inside the 3 TB included with the server.
- **Limits of the test:** everything ran on one PC (server, Postgres, storage and the robots
  together). Place discussions, guests and the admin portal weren't included; the voice and
  video files are synthetic but sized like real recordings.

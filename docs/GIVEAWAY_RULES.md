## JaxSide Launch Giveaway: Official Rules

**NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning.**

### 1. Sponsor

This giveaway is run by **[JaxSide LLC, address of the registered agent]** ("JaxSide", "we").
It is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Apple Inc. or Google LLC.
Apple and Google are not responsible for it, and you are providing information to JaxSide, not
to them.

### 2. Entry period

From **[start date and time]** to **[end date and time]**, Eastern Time (the "Entry Period").

### 3. Who can enter

- You must be **18 or older**.
- You must use the JaxSide app inside **Duval County, Florida** (the app already checks this).
- You must have **one** JaxSide account. Only one entry per person.
- Employees of JaxSide and their immediate family are not eligible.

### 4. How to enter

During the Entry Period:

1. Create a free JaxSide account and **confirm your email**.
2. Make at least **[N]** posts on **[at least M different days]**.

Posts must be your own, follow the Community Guidelines, and still be visible on the
Entry Period's last day. Posts that were deleted or removed by moderators do not count.
Duplicate, copied, empty or automated posts do not count.

There is no other way to enter. Your account's activity is your entry; there is nothing to
fill in. We count qualifying posts from our own records, and our count is final.

### 5. Disqualification

We may disqualify any account, and its entry, that:

- breaks the Terms of Service or Community Guidelines, including being banned or suspended
  at any point in the Entry Period or before the drawing;
- is a duplicate account, a bot, or posts only to meet the requirement (spam);
- uses a false or temporary email address.

### 6. The drawing

Within **[X]** days after the Entry Period ends, we will pick **[one / number]** winner at random
from all eligible accounts, using a random number generator. Each eligible account has the same
chance. Your odds depend on the number of eligible entries received.

### 7. Prize

**[One]** **[$ amount]** **[brand]** gift card, sent by email. Approximate retail value:
**[$ amount]**. No cash alternative, no substitution, and the prize can't be transferred.
Winners are responsible for any taxes on the prize.

### 8. Winner notification

The winner is told in the JaxSide app and by email to the address on the account. The winner
must, within **[5]** days:

- confirm that the account is still active and theirs, and
- confirm that the email address on the account is where the gift card should be sent.

If the winner does not answer in time, is not eligible, or the email can't receive the prize,
we may pick another winner the same way. The gift card is sent only to the email address on the
winning account.

### 9. Your information

We use your email only to run this giveaway (to contact the winner and send the prize). Your
username is the only thing that could be shared publicly. We will not publish your real name or
email. See the JaxSide Privacy Policy.

### 10. General

- We may change, suspend or end the giveaway if something outside our control (a technical
  failure, fraud, or a legal problem) affects it fairly running.
- Our decisions on eligibility and the winner are final.
- By entering you agree to these rules.
- Void where prohibited. Governed by Florida law.
- **Winners list:** the winner's username is available on request to **[support email]** for
  **[60]** days after the drawing.
